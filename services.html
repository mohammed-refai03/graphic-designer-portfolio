14 // SCOPE TRANSPARENCY

WHAT'S INCLUDED & WHAT'S NOT

Complete clarity on deliverables, revisions, source files, and boundary limits for all commissions.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

GUARANTEED DELIVERABLES
  • Full Master Vector Files: EPS, SVG, AI, and High-Res CMYK print PDFs.
  • Commercial Intellectual Property: 100% full copyright handoff upon final payment.
  • Structured Revision Rounds: 2 to 3 dedicated revision cycles per project milestone.
  • Brand Guidelines Monograph: Color specs, typography kerning rules, and usage guides.
  • Packaging Dieline Specifications: Foil stamp CAD layers, spot UV masks, and stock guides.
  • Direct Art Director Sync: Direct communication with Stackly (no middle management).

WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED

OUT OF SCOPE / EXTRA
  • Font Software Licenses: Client is responsible for purchasing commercial font desktop licenses.
  • Third-Party Print Costs: Printing house press runs, physical paper stocks, and shipping.
  • Stock Photo & Video Rights: Third-party stock royalty licenses beyond included assets.
  • Unrestricted Scope Creep: Substantial structural redesigns after concept phase sign-off.
  • Web Hosting & Server Setup: Domain registration, DNS hosting, and backend database administration.
  • Copywriting & Content Creation: Written text copy must be supplied by the client.

16 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SERVICE & PRICING FAQ

Answers regarding payment terms, revisions, turnarounds, and contract details.

01

How are payments structured for design commissions?

Commissions are structured on a 50/50 payment milestone basis: 50% deposit upon contract initiation to reserve capacity, and 50% upon final sign-off before transmission of full master open vector file vaults.

02

What is the turnaround time for each service tier?

Single discipline deliverables (packaging dieline, poster suite) take 2–3 weeks. Full Brand Architecture systems take 4–6 weeks. Retainer Art Direction runs on agreed monthly sprints.

03

Do I own the full intellectual property rights to the final designs?

Yes. Upon final payment completion, 100% full commercial copyright ownership of all custom-designed vector assets, marks, dielines, and guidelines is transferred completely to your company.

04

Can I request custom service bundles or monthly retainers?

Absolutely. If your venture requires a bespoke combination (e.g. Brand Identity + Packaging + Motion Titles), we craft a custom scoped proposal with unified milestone pricing.
