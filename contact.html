11 // CLARIFICATIONS & POLICIES

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions regarding timelines, pricing model, revision policies, and international remote workflow.

FAQ #01 TIMELINES & SCHEDULE

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Full brand identity architectures typically take 2–4 weeks. Editorial monograph publications take 1–2 weeks. Motion graphics and 3D visual staging require 2–3 weeks. Fast-track rush production slots are available upon consultation.

FAQ #02 PRICING & DEPOSIT

How does your pricing & payment structure work?

We operate on a transparent fixed-fee project rate. Terms require a 50% deposit upon brief sign-off to lock production schedule, with the final 50% due upon master asset package transmission.

FAQ #03 REVISIONS & FEEDS

How many revision iterations are included?

Every commission includes 3 rounds of comprehensive revisions per milestone. Our initial design discovery alignment ensures visual direction is locked before final vector rendering.

FAQ #04 REMOTE WORKFLOW

Do you collaborate with international clients remotely?

Yes! Over 80% of our commissions originate from global clients in Tokyo, London, New York, and Berlin. All communication runs seamlessly through Slack, Zoom, and video briefs.

FAQ #05 ASSET OWNERSHIP

Who owns the final master source files and intellectual property?

Upon final balance clearance, 100% full commercial copyright and master open vector files (.AI, .EPS, .SVG, 3D CAD dielines, and font licenses) are transferred directly to your organization.

FAQ #06 DELIVERABLES

What file formats are provided upon project completion?

You receive a complete production asset vault containing scalable vectors, print-ready CMYK PDF dielines, 4K WebM/MP4 motion loops, and web-optimized dark mode asset packages.
