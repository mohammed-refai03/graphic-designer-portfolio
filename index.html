CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers regarding design process, timelines, deliverables, and commissioning.

01

What specific design services do you specialize in?

I specialize in brand identity architecture, geometric vector logo matrices, 2D editorial publication design, kinetic typography systems, luxury packaging dieline CAD, and digital UI component grids.

02

What is your typical project timeline?

Timelines depend on scope. Focused packaging dielines or logo packages take approximately 2–3 weeks, while comprehensive brand identity systems, publication books, and motion campaign directions take 4–6 weeks.

03

How do revisions and feedback iterations work?

Every commission includes 2 to 3 structured revision rounds. We review interactive PDF proofs, vector wireframe sheets, and high-resolution mockups to refine every kerning detail and color ratio before final file delivery.

04

What deliverables do I receive upon completion?

You receive complete production asset vaults including editable SVG/EPS vector source files, high-resolution CMYK print dieline PDFs, font usage guidelines, digital WebGL assets, and full copyright ownership handoff.

05

How do we initiate a project together?

Simply click the "Start a Project" button or email info@stackly.com with your project scope, target timeline, and estimated budget. I will review your requirements and respond within 24 hours with a custom proposal.
